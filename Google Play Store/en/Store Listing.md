# Full description

My Logger – event tracker & smart reminders

Do you ever wonder: when was the last time you did something? Or how often does it actually happen?

My Logger is a simple event tracker / journal that lets you quickly log events, track their frequency, and explore statistics and charts — without planning and without unnecessary complexity.

WHAT DOES THE APP DO?

✔ add entries in seconds
✔ see when something last happened
✔ track how often things occur
✔ view a clear and simple timeline
✔ discover patterns through visual statistics and charts

STATISTICS & CHARTS

Turn your event history into meaningful insights.

✔ explore your activity with easy-to-read bar charts
✔ track trends over time with a line chart
✔ analyze event frequency in 7, 30, or 365-day periods
✔ see how your average number of logs changes over time

Whether you log something daily or just a few times a year, visual statistics help you understand your patterns at a glance.

SMART REMINDERS (HOURGLASSES)

Set reminders that work differently than in typical apps.

✔ count time from your last entry
✔ automatically reset after each new log
✔ support fully custom intervals (e.g. 10 days, 5 months, 3 years)
✔ highlight what is already “due”

No need to manually reschedule anything — the app adapts to you.

WHAT CAN YOU USE IT FOR?

• habit tracking (without streak pressure)
• reminders for irregular tasks
• tracking event frequency and trends
• a simple event journal

WHY THIS APP?

✔ maximum simplicity
✔ fast logging
✔ visual statistics and insights
✔ no unnecessary features or clutter
✔ flexible reminders

Stay in control of your time and habits — without complications.
